How it works

From vague idea to solid plan.

The same Friday night, step by step. The examples show the experience we’re designing, not a finished app.

  1. 01

    Say what you’re after

    Describe the outing the way you’d text it. No filters, no forms.

    RRohan, for the usual four

    something new on friday after 7, nothing too pricey

  2. 02

    Planic weighs everyone together

    Each person’s tastes and limits are designed to shape the result, and you can see how.

    • AVeg options→Only places with a veg menu
    • RSomewhere new→The usual café is set aside
    • M₹600 max→The rooftop lounge is set aside
    • MFrom Noida→Start moves from 7 to 7:30
  3. 03

    Get one plan you can act on

    One suggestion with its reasons. Go with it, tweak it, or send it to the group.

    The planFri · 7:30 PM

    Board games, then momos

    Hauz Khas · about ₹550 each

    Sounds goodSwap a stopSend to group
