Privacy

Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated October 2026

What we collect

This website only collects what you choose to send us:

  • Your email address, if you join the early-access list.
  • Your name, email and message, if you contact us.

There are no accounts, no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies on this site.

How we use it

We use your email only to tell you about Planic — mainly when early access is ready — and to reply to messages you send. We don’t sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Where it’s stored

Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, which hosts this website. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your email from the list, or to delete anything you’ve sent us, at any time through the contact page. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe.

Changes

Planic is an early-stage product. When the app launches, this policy will be updated to cover it, and we’ll let people on the list know.
