## The plan

- **When:** Friday, starting at 7:30 PM. The meeting point is near the metro at 7:20.
- **What:** board games, then momos nearby, from 9:30.
- **Where:** Hauz Khas (an area, not a named venue).
- **Cost:** about ₹550 each.
- **Trade-off:** Meera has the longest trip, so the plan starts at 7:30 rather than 7.

## Rules for using it

- No fit percentages or scores. Show reasons and trade-offs instead.
- No claims of group consensus ("4/4 in", "everybody's in"). The plan is something the group can accept, tweak or send.
- No travel times in minutes. The page only says who has the longest trip.
- Label product UI as a concept or illustrative example wherever it appears.
